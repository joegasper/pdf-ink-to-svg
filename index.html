Turn the handwriting in a PDF into SVG

Drop in a PDF that was signed or marked up with a pen or stylus. Each drawing comes out as a crisp, scalable SVG you can download — including handwriting that has been flattened into the page.

Your files stay on your device. Everything happens in this browser tab: the PDF is never uploaded and nothing is sent anywhere. The only things kept between visits are your theme and language choices.

Extracted ink

Results marked Flattened were found by looking for hand-drawn-looking strokes in the page itself. Dense vector graphics, such as a chart line, can look the same — check the preview before downloading.
